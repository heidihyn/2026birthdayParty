Pour your own

  1. Grab a cup at the bar.
  2. Kill Switch or Hallucination: fill the cup about a third of the way from the labeled bottle. The vodka's already in there.
  3. Sandbox Mode: fill it halfway from the bottle marked 0%, then add a splash of the cranberry marked 0%.
  4. Top it off with a can of Sprite.
  5. Give the bottle a gentle swirl first. The glitter settles.

These are strong. Want it lighter? Pour less and add more Sprite. Zero-proof bottles are marked 0% and sit on their own corner of the bar.

Water is at the door and the bar. Hydration is mandatory.
