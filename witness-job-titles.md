# Witness job titles

Every Witness gets one unique Heartware job title. The title is flavor only: the clue and the rules are the same for all of them ("You know for sure that [name] is NOT a Hacker"; wins if a Hacker is fired).

**Keep titles private.** A title is only ever shown to the guest who holds it (their card / DM). Never list guest names next to titles in public (roster, site, slides): only Witnesses have titles, so a public title would prove that person is innocent and anyone without one is a special role.
